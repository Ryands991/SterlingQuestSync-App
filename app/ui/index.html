Looking for WoW...
Made for WoW Forever
Sterling Quest Sync and Sterling are built and tested for WoW Forever. With another version of WoW they may not work as expected.

The app only adds a small SterlingQuestSync add-on folder (and Sterling, if you tick the box). It never writes to WTF or any other add-on.

Is this your first PC with Sterling Quest Sync?

Lost your other PC? Use a recovery code instead.

STEP 2 OF 3

Link to your other PC

On your other PC, open Settings > Link another PC. Type the code it shows. It lasts 10 minutes.

Your partners carry over. Nothing is removed from your other PC.

STEP 2 OF 3

Use your recovery code

It starts with SQS-. Your account and partners come back on this PC.

STEP 3 OF 3, OPTIONAL

Your recovery code

If you ever lose every PC with this app, this code gets your account and partners back. Keep it somewhere safe, like a password manager. Shown once.

  • Skipping is fine. Without it, a fresh install just starts a new account and you pair with friends again.
  • Download saves a small text file wherever you pick, named sterling-quest-sync-recovery.txt.
  • You can make a new code any time in Settings. The old one stops working.

The server doesn't know this PC

Your account may have expired after a long break, been deleted, or this PC was removed from it. Your quests in game are safe. Nothing on this PC was changed.
