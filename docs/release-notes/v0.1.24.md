## What's new

- New names: the add-on is now Sterling Quest Sync, and this app is its Companion.
- The app notices when WoW didn't load the Companion add-on and tells you how to fix it.
- If WowUp or the CurseForge app keeps the add-on updated, the Companion leaves it to them.
- New in Settings: Start with Windows, favourite notifications and Copy details for help.
- Invite a friend who doesn't have the app from their row on the Partners page.

## Install or update

- **New or updating:** download `SterlingQuestSync_0.1.24_x64-setup.exe` below and run it. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Your settings and partners stay.
- The Companion is optional and works with the [Sterling Quest Sync](https://github.com/SterlingAddons/SterlingQuest) add-on. Setup, troubleshooting and privacy: [the app's page](https://github.com/SterlingAddons/SterlingQuestSync-App#readme).

`latest.json` and the `.sig` file are for the app's own updater; you don't need to download them.
